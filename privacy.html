Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 26, 2026

About FrontBoard

FrontBoard is a travel-document display service hosted on GitHub Pages. Travel-document extraction runs in your browser. If you sign in with Google and accept the storage notice, FrontBoard stores the original document and extracted travel details in a private Supabase project.

Guest use without Google sign-in

You may upload CSV, JSON, TXT, and PDF documents without Google sign-in. Guest extraction runs locally in your browser. FrontBoard does not send guest documents to its storage service. Records remain in browser memory and are removed when you clear them or reload/close the page unless you separately save or download them.

Google sign-in

Google Sign-In is used to identify your account through Supabase Auth. The Google ID token is sent to Supabase Auth for verification; it is not saved in local storage. Sign-in does not request access to Google Drive. FrontBoard keeps your name and email address in your browser's local storage for the account display. The Supabase auth session is held in browser memory only, so you must sign in again after reloading the page.

Cloud storage consent

Before the first cloud upload for your Google account, FrontBoard displays a notice explaining that the original document and extracted travel details will be stored in FrontBoard's private cloud storage. You must affirmatively check the consent box and accept. If you decline, the document is parsed locally and is not uploaded.

After consent, the original document and its related JSON record are stored in the private frontboard-private Supabase Storage bucket under a prefix derived from your Supabase user ID. The bucket is not configured for public access. Supabase Row Level Security policies restrict each account to its own consent record, document metadata, and storage objects. A consent version and timestamp are retained to enforce and document your choice. FrontBoard does not use Google Drive scopes or store documents in your Drive.

Extracted and sensitive data

Travel documents may contain names, ages, ticket numbers, routes, dates, times, seats, fares, booking references, and other personal information. Extracted fields and supporting evidence are stored in the JSON record paired with the original document after you accept cloud storage. The same personal information is visible in the FrontBoard interface.

How data is used

  • To identify and display travel records.
  • To store your uploaded originals and extracted JSON records in your private FrontBoard storage.
  • To provide confidence scores and review flags for extracted fields.
  • To maintain session state in browser storage.

FrontBoard does not sell personal data or use uploaded documents for advertising.

Sharing and providers

Google provides account authentication, Supabase provides authentication, database, and private file storage, and GitHub Pages hosts the interface. PDF parsing and document extraction run in the browser through FrontBoard code and PDF.js. Review Google's Privacy Policy and Supabase's Privacy Policy for their processing terms.

Retention and deletion

Guest records remain only in browser memory. Cloud-stored documents and their JSON records remain in your FrontBoard storage until you delete them. Use the Delete action beside a saved document to remove the original, associated JSON record, and document metadata. The consent record is retained. Clearing the local board or signing out does not delete cloud files. Supabase's provider-level retention, if any, is governed by its terms.

Your choices and rights

You may decline Google sign-in and continue as a guest, or decline the storage notice and keep processing in your browser. You may sign out and delete individual saved documents at any time. Google account permissions can be reviewed at Google Account Permissions.

Security

FrontBoard uses HTTPS, Supabase Auth, a private Storage bucket, and Row Level Security policies to isolate each account's files and metadata. The browser uses only the project's public publishable key; no service-role key is shipped to the browser. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Do not upload highly sensitive documents on a public or shared computer.

Children

FrontBoard is not directed to children, and users should avoid uploading children's information unless authorized by a parent or guardian.

Changes

We may update this policy to reflect app or legal changes. The effective date will identify the latest revision.
