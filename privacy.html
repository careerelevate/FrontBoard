Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026

About FrontBoard

FrontBoard is a travel-document display service hosted on GitHub Pages. It can process uploaded travel documents locally in the browser or, when you authorize it, store documents and extracted JSON records in your Google Drive.

Guest use without Google sign-in

You may upload CSV, JSON, TXT, and PDF documents without Google sign-in. Guest extraction runs locally in your browser. FrontBoard does not send guest documents to its own server. Records remain in browser memory and are removed when you clear them or reload/close the page unless you separately save or download them.

Google sign-in

If you choose Google Sign-In, Google provides your account name, email address, profile identifier, and profile image as permitted by your Google account and consent choices. FrontBoard stores this information in your browser's local storage only to keep the account name visible and personalize the interface.

Google Drive access

With your consent, FrontBoard requests access to create and manage the FrontBoard folder and files used by this application. The app creates or finds FrontBoard / year / month folders and stores uploaded originals and normalized JSON records there. Files are written to your Google Drive. FrontBoard cannot access unrelated Drive files unless a broader scope has been separately granted.

Extracted and sensitive data

Travel documents may contain names, ages, ticket numbers, routes, dates, times, seats, fares, booking references, and other personal information. Extracted fields are shown to you and may be stored in JSON in your Drive when you sign in. Evidence text is limited to the data needed for the record and may contain identifying information.

How data is used

  • To identify and display travel records.
  • To store your uploaded originals and extracted JSON records in your Drive.
  • To provide confidence scores and review flags for extracted fields.
  • To maintain session state in browser storage.

FrontBoard does not sell personal data or use uploaded documents for advertising.

Sharing and providers

Google handles authentication and Drive access under Google's privacy terms. PDF parsing and document extraction run in the browser through FrontBoard code and PDF.js. A future server integration is not assumed or enabled by this policy without a clear notice and authorization.

Retention and deletion

Guest records remain only in browser memory. Signed-in records remain in your Google Drive until you delete them. You may sign out, clear the page's records, remove browser local storage, or delete FrontBoard folders/files directly in Google Drive.

Your choices and rights

You may decline Google sign-in and continue as a guest. You may revoke FrontBoard access through your Google Account at Google Account Permissions. You may request questions or deletion guidance by email.

Security

FrontBoard uses HTTPS, OAuth access tokens, and least-privilege access where available. No browser application can guarantee absolute security. Do not upload highly sensitive documents on a public or shared computer.

Children

FrontBoard is not directed to children, and users should avoid uploading children's information unless authorized by a parent or guardian.

Changes

We may update this policy to reflect app or legal changes. The effective date will identify the latest revision.
